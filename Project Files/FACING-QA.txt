Mochi pointer-facing update - version 1.1.8

When the nearby pointer turns Mochi left or right, that visible gaze direction now
becomes his remembered idle direction. Moving beyond tracking range, or back over
his body, no longer turns him back to his earlier direction. Straight up/down
poses retain the previous side, including small pointer movements around vertical.
A pointer already outside tracking range cannot change his facing.

The change only records the current gaze pose's horizontal side. It does not
reschedule swimming, Idle Activities, or Playful Mode. Existing smoothing and
vertical-direction hysteresis remain in use. Interactions and travel still set
their own facing as before.

Regression coverage uses the actual companion timer and idle renderer: all 16
pointer directions, both starting facings, all three sizes, and Playful Mode on
and off. It covers distant and close exits, vertical jitter, continuous reversals,
and unchanged activity deadlines. The new check fails against the old behavior.

Validation: all 22 regression groups passed under Wine (10 companion, 7 updater,
5 Playful Mode). A live nonactivating companion also passed real pointer movement
from right-facing idle to a leftward look and left-facing idle, then a rightward
look and right-facing idle. Reviewed the rendered poses after both departures.
The staged, root, and preview ZIP executables match; update.xml records their
version, size, and SHA-256. Compiled against Microsoft .NET Framework 4.8 reference
assemblies. A live Windows desktop check remains for the preview user.

Earlier directional animation update (historical notes)
-------------------------------------------------------
Mochi directional animation update - 30 September 2026

Implemented: left/right idle memory; all original 13 reactions mirrored in both directions; three new right-facing routines (snack toss, nose boop, bubble surf). Double-click chooses among 29 directional variants. Manual menus retain their own saved cycles; idle activities still rotate feed/pet/play every 3-5 minutes.

Swimming records the horizontal direction from its start to its destination. Dragging records the latest horizontal movement, including reversals. Pure vertical movement retains the previous side. Each interaction sets its own side and retains it after completion or interruption. Cursor gaze temporarily looks around without overwriting that memory.

Each launch chooses a random safe position from the available monitor working areas and either the left-facing or right-facing normal idle pose. Introductions and the following controls hint keep that direction until movement or an interaction changes it.

Validation: complete self-test suite passed, including all 29 directional variants through the actual handlers/timer, 100 swims, drag reversals/releases, temporary gaze, 3,000 random placements across landscape/portrait/negative-coordinate monitors, both idle startup directions, readable speech, and render bounds at all three sizes. Mirrored body pixel comparisons allow GDI+'s one-pixel raster registration difference. Prior single-pose and no-seam rendering checks passed.

Visual review: inspected rendered left/right idle and barrel-roll/backflip pairs, both idle startup directions, and contact sheets of the three new routines against a dark background. Confirmed speech is not mirrored, boop effects remain visible, snack follows its mirrored arc, and bubble surfing uses a single crisp body image.

Limit: native UI automation cannot target the nonactivating borderless pet, so interaction behavior was tested using the real handlers and timer in simulation, with actual renderer output inspected separately.
