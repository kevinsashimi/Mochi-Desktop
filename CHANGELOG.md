# What's New in Mochi

Small updates for your little ocean. These notes are also available offline in Settings → What's New.

## 1.2.11 - A softer sun over Quiet Cove

### A little golden calm
- Quiet Cove now has one larger sunlight opening, sitting a little lower on the ocean surface with a soft golden glow and gentle rays.
- Removed the second bright patch that looked like another sun. The detailed reef, seabed and repaired rocks remain in place.
- The wallpaper stays static and optional, with a full 3840 × 2160 painting that fills a 4K UHD display without stretching or zooming.

## 1.2.10 - Bubble-powered zoomies!

### Tiny shark, turbo fins
- Tap Space in Manual mode for a short, speedy boost, with a five-second recharge shown beneath Mochi.
- Leave a swirling trail of sparkling bubbles, with the occasional heart bubble, faster fin wiggles and sometimes a playful quip.
- Three new illustrated boost animations bring a rocket grin, an eyes-closed “wheee!” and a wink that turns into a giggle. His fins kick and relax in both directions, with a brief happy settle after the dash and no immediately repeated expression.

### A little room to zoom
- Boost in your steering direction, or your last swimming direction when resting. Screen edges still keep Mochi safe.
- Holding Space does not repeat boosts. Leaving and re-entering Manual mode keeps the remaining cooldown; other activities still wait their turn.
- Swim right to every edge of the screen in Manual mode. Empty space around Mochi and his speech bubble no longer acts as an invisible barrier.
- A tidy controls card lists swimming, boosting and exiting on separate lines. It gently fades after you start using the controls; move your mouse to see it again. The card moves out of Mochi's path when he approaches.
- Settings explains automatic roaming, with Swim frequency directly underneath. Turning off Swim around occasionally greys out its frequency control while remembering your choice.

## 1.2.9 - You're the captain!

### A swim at your fingertips
- Choose Manual mode in Mochi's right-click menu and steer with WASD or the arrow keys, including diagonal swims.
- A familiar instruction card guides you. Press Escape, right-click, or switch apps to finish.

### Room for every little adventure
- Smooth starts, stops and turns keep Mochi inside the current screen, even with automatic swimming paused.
- Idle Activities and Playful Mode wait while you steer. Pending turns keep their deadlines and resume with a three-second pause.

- Quiet Cove remains a static 3840 × 2160 wallpaper, with no stretching or zooming on a 4K UHD display.

## 1.2.7 - A wider world for Quiet Cove (preview)

### More ocean to explore
- Quiet Cove now fills a 3840 × 2160 desktop with new scenery, without stretching or zooming the artwork.

## 1.2.6 - Quiet Cove, a fresh start (preview)

### A peaceful place for Mochi
- A completely new static wallpaper: sunlit limestone arches, a winding sandy path, soft corals and tranquil blue water.
- Quiet Cove starts off. Turn it on in Settings and choose Save whenever Mochi needs a change of scenery.

### Still waters
- Removed the live wallpaper engine and its artwork from the app. Mochi's own animations and activities continue as usual.
- Earlier live-wallpaper preferences do not enable this new option. Turning it off or quitting Mochi reveals your usual background.
- The full 1672 × 941 painting keeps its proportions, with a blue surround on larger screens and proportional reduction on smaller ones. It is not enlarged or cropped.

## 1.2.2 - Opening the ocean's window (preview)

## 1.2.1 - Bringing the ocean into view (preview)

### A trip down memory lane

## 1.2.0 - An ocean to call home (preview)

### New
- Mochi has a living reef to swim in: drifting fish, a visiting sea turtle, swaying sea fans, bubbles, and soft sunbeams. Ocean live wallpaper is on by default.
- Prefer your usual wallpaper? Turn off Ocean live wallpaper in Settings and choose Save. Quitting Mochi also reveals your existing wallpaper.

### Made for your screen
- The ocean renders at each monitor's native resolution, including 4K, ultrawide, and portrait layouts. Reef artwork keeps its proportions and is never upscaled; wider screens have more open water.
- The ocean sits behind desktop icons and leaves your wallpaper file and desktop items unchanged. It pauses behind a covering app or while the session is locked, and runs more gently on battery.
- Settings now scrolls on smaller screens and at larger display scales, with Save and Cancel always within reach.

### Good to know
- Requires the standard Windows Explorer desktop and an OpenGL 2.1 capable display driver. If either is unavailable, your usual wallpaper stays visible and Mochi keeps swimming.

## 1.1.9 - Fresh out of the tide!

## 1.1.8 - A look that lingers

### Improved
- After following your pointer, Mochi keeps facing the last side he looked toward when it moves away. Left stays left; right stays right.
- Looking straight up or down preserves his previous side. This works with Playful Mode on or off, without resetting activity timers.

## 1.1.7 - A little more breathing room

### Fixed
- Click outside Mochi's right-click menu or press Escape to close it without choosing anything.
- The Playful Mode description in Settings has enough room for every letter, including at larger display scales.

## 1.1.6 - Smoother sailing for updates

### Fixed
- Fixed a Windows .NET Framework compatibility problem that could stop an update from installing.
- Update errors now explain which step failed and save a local diagnostic report. Info shows the running app's location.

### Updating an older copy
- If an older updater fails, quit Mochi and replace only Mochi.exe with a newer copy. Keep your settings and Project Files folder.

## 1.1.5 - More little visits

### Improved
- Idle Activities now happen every 1–3 minutes: a snack, a pat, then a little playtime.
- Playful Mode keeps its own 3–5 minute clock. Activities wait their turn, with a three-second breather between queued animations.

## 1.1.4 - Tiny fins, a little mischief

## Early days - How a little whale shark became Mochi

These milestones predate numbered releases and were recovered from the original development conversations and repository history.

### 11–12 September 2026 · Hello, tiny fins
- Mochi began as a little whale shark pet for Codex, with his first illustrated animations and 16 directions to look around.

### 26 September 2026 · A desktop buddy
- A standalone Windows app brought Mochi onto the desktop: drag him around, pet him, feed a tiny snack, or play together. Settings added size and swim frequency choices, plus an option to start with Windows.
- Feeding grew into a rotation of snack chases, happy rolls, and tummy pats. Speech bubbles, gentle breathing, and pointer watching gave him more personality.
- Nuzzles, flipper hugs, barrel rolls, backflips, and other illustrated reactions expanded his collection of moves.

### 30 September 2026 · Finding his rhythm
- Automatic Idle Activities began rotating through snacks, pats, and playtime. They originally ran every 3–5 minutes; version 1.1.5 later shortened this to 1–3 minutes.
- Swimming gained straight and curved paths, gentler starts and stops, and better border avoidance. Cleaner pose transitions removed doubled images and visible seams.
- Left- and right-facing reactions, snack tosses, nose boops, and bubble surfing brought the collection to 16 routines with 32 directional variants.
- Single-click surprises, drag-and-drop quips, startup greetings, and a safe random starting spot made everyday visits more fun. Mochi settled behind normal app windows.

### 30 September–1 October 2026 · A little adventure
- Come here gained a destination picker: choose a spot on any monitor and watch Mochi swim over. Escape or right-click cancels the invitation.
- New departure and arrival lines celebrate the journey. Tiny fins, big mission!
