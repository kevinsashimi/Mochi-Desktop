Mochi interaction and swimming update - 30 September 2026

- A single left-click chooses a random reaction across feeding, petting, and play immediately on mouse release. No double-click handler or delay remains. Repeated rapid clicks are ordinary individual clicks. Drag releases do not trigger reactions. Menu category cycles remain independent.
- The startup hint reads: Click to interact with me! Right-click for more.
- Removed Come here from the context menu. A single tray-icon click opens the controls; double-click summoning was removed.
- Added six pickup sayings and six landing sayings, each selected randomly without consecutive repeats.
- All 16 routines now have both directions, including snack toss, nose boop, and bubble surf. Both-direction enumeration and render checks apply automatically to future additions. Reaction bodies and effects are mirrored as complete images, then readable speech is drawn afterward.
- Swimming randomly selects straight, curved, or mixed paths. Mixed paths combine a line and a curve in either order with matching heading at their join. An arc-length lookup preserves continuous speed through joins; gradual launch/landing remains. Curve controls are kept within each monitor's usable area. Border escape remains active.

Existing single-pose/no-seam checks passed.
